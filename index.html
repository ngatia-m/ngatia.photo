About

I'm an economist and a street and documentary photographer from Nairobi, Kenya. I photograph unguarded moments — the ordinary things people do when they aren't performing for anyone — and I'm most interested in the patterns underneath them.

That pulls me toward markets, port towns, Chinatowns and fishing communities at first light: places where the same small human choreography repeats every day and rewards standing still long enough to see it. I work in hard light and high colour, and I'd rather wait for a frame to assemble itself than arrange one.
